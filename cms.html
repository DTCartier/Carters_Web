How it works

Three steps to a live change

No software to install and nothing to learn beyond filling in a form.

  1. 01

    Log in on your own site

    Go to yourdomain.com/admin and sign in. You only see your own website.

  2. 02

    Edit in sections

    Pages are built from ready-made sections: banners, text, photos, feature lists and calls to action. Preview before anything goes live.

  3. 03

    Publish

    Click Publish and your site is rebuilt automatically. Changes are live in about a minute.
